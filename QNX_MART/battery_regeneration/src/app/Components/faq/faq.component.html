Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about company registration, product listings, commissions, and our partner referral program

Registering your company on our platform is a streamlined, fully digital process. The registration fee is a one-time payment of ₹1000. This covers your company's verification, dashboard access, and onboarding support. You’ll need to provide standard business documents such as GSTIN, PAN, and bank account details for settlements. Once verified, you can immediately start managing your product catalog.

Product listing on our B2B marketplace is completely free. There are no per-listing fees, no hidden upload charges, and no restrictions on the number of SKUs you can add. You retain full control over your inventory, pricing, and product descriptions without worrying about upfront listing costs eating into your margins.

We don't follow a one-size-fits-all commission model. Instead, the commission structure is mutually agreed upon during your onboarding process. We draft a customized agreement per company that takes into account your product category, estimated sales volume, and specific business requirements. This ensures a fair, transparent, and flexible partnership tailored to your operational goals.

Our Partner Program allows you to earn by referring other businesses to the platform. Once you refer a company and they successfully register (and start operations), you are eligible for a referral commission. The commission applies to both their registration fee and a share of the sales commission generated by that referred company, all as defined in your specific partner agreement. You can track all your referrals and earnings directly from your dashboard.

Once you submit a referral, the referred company receives a dedicated onboarding link. You can monitor their registration status in real-time through your partner dashboard. Upon their successful registration, verification, and activation on the platform, the referral commission is calculated and credited to your account as per the terms of your partner agreement. We ensure complete visibility into every stage of the referral lifecycle.

We understand that every business operates differently. Our agreements are not rigid contracts; they are designed to accommodate diverse business models, product verticals, and partnership scopes. Whether you are a manufacturer, distributor, or service provider, we outline clear terms regarding commission splits, payout cycles, and referral rewards in a formal agreement that reflects our mutual understanding and protects the interests of all parties involved.
